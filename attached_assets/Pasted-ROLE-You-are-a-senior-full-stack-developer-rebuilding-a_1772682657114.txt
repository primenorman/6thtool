[4] DAY 1 FAST TRACK CONTENT STRUCTURE
Create a new module called "Day 0: Fast Track" that sits ABOVE Module 1 in the course list.
This is accessible immediately without completing anything else.

Lesson 0.1 — "The Operating System Concept" (~10 min read, structured as below):
  Layout: 60-second hook → Core concept → One action → Optional deep dive (collapsed)
  Hook (large text, visible immediately): "Your brain is hardware. You've been running
  the wrong software. Today you install the right one."
  Core concept: 3 short paragraphs max on the bio-computer metaphor. Use the analogy:
  "Most mental training tells you to 'try harder.' That's like deleting files to speed up
  a slow computer. We go to the source code."
  Action: "Rate your mental performance confidence right now: [slider 1-10]"
  This logs their first Certainty Rating baseline.
  Optional deep dive: [collapsed accordion] Full Cybernetic Transposition explanation.

Lesson 0.2 — "Your First 15-Minute Practice" (~15 min guided):
  This is the daily routine preview. Structure:
  - Step 1 (3 min): "Find a quiet spot. Close your eyes. Take 3 deep breaths."
    Display a simple breath timer (4 counts in, 7 hold, 8 out)
  - Step 2 (5 min): "Think of your best recent at-bat. One specific moment.
    Write 3 words that describe how you felt." → Text input, saved to their profile.
  - Step 3 (5 min): "Now rate these on a 1-10 slider:" [Confidence / Focus / Readiness]
  - Step 4 (2 min): "Your mission for tomorrow: do this exact practice again.
    Same time, same spot." → Ask for preferred reminder time, save to profile.
  On completion: Award 50 XP, show "PRACTICE COMPLETE 🔥 Streak: 1 day"

Lesson 0.3 — "The Certainty Rating System" (~5 min):
  Explain the 1-10 Certainty Rating as their "system health monitor."
  Show a simple chart of what each number means in baseball terms:
  1-3: "System needs a reboot" | 4-6: "System is running, but lagging" |
  7-8: "System is optimized" | 9-10: "Elite mode — locked in"
  Action: Log today's overall Certainty Rating.
