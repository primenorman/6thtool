### SECTION 3: THE FOUR INTENTIONS PROTOCOL

  "execution sequence":

    2. Hit the ball hard (Process Focus Override)

    4. Help the team win (Ego Dissolution / Collective Purpose Load)

  override of fear-based subroutines
- Include an animated "Loading Sequence" visual that simulates 
  running these four intentions like a software boot sequence,

### SECTION 4: OPENING DAY MINDSET
- Title: "Temporal Decoupling: The Art of Having No Yesterday"

### SECTION 5: HUNTING SPEEDS — PREDICTIVE PROCESSING
